## 1. Scientific position

**Retain–Forget–Revise (RFR) is a core, falsifiable *computational hypothesis* within the existing ContraMamba program, not a replacement of its long-term objective, an established mechanism, or an architecture to implement immediately.** It explicitly extends the original corrective-state problem: how can a resource-bounded recurrent state preserve information needed for future decisions, suppress information that should no longer influence them, and *revise* beliefs and their dependent consequences when evidence changes?

This is continuous with the frozen long-term order:

1. Discover what existing native recurrent computation represents and changes, including normal judgments and suspected error precursors.
2. Localize and causally test which updates participate in those functions.
3. Test selective interventions, restoration, suppression, and (later, separately authorized) formation control during learning.
4. Identify which computations are necessary, sufficient, substitutable, or redundant, and *why* an intervention worked.
5. Only when warranted, use validated principles to inform future general-purpose intelligence architectures.

A null precursor finding does not block RFR: the correct preservation and use of non-error information is itself testable.

## 2. Three different functional requirements

| Requirement | Functional question | Potential failure | Stronger-than-observation criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Retain** | Which evidence, relations, and computations remain useful for future tasks? | Required evidence decays or becomes inaccessible | The retained content causally supports correct *delayed* decisions, under matched distractors and durations |
| **Forget** | Which prior influence should cease to affect future decisions? | Irrelevant, obsolete, or contradicted influence contaminates judgment | Targeted reduction of that influence without collateral loss of useful, unrelated functions |
| **Revise** | How should a new, sufficiently grounded contradiction update a belief **and the decisions dependent on it**? | A stale belief or derived conclusion survives; unrelated knowledge is overwritten | Verified revision of the original relation *and* its dependent consequences, with unrelated-function preservation |

"Forget" need not mean erasing a coordinate, and "Retain" need not mean leaving an activation unchanged. Information may be transformed or redistributed before being functionally preserved. A decision to abstain (End/Defer) is a separate readout/authorization issue, not a synonym for forgetting.

## 3. Conceptual mathematics — not a tested new recurrence

A candidate decomposition is

\[
\boxed{s_{t+1}=r_t\odot s_t+w_t\odot\phi(x_t)+c_t\odot\Delta_{\mathrm{revise}}(s_t,x_t)}.
\]

Here \(r_t\), \(w_t\), and \(c_t\) are abstract factors for prior-state carry/attenuation, writing, and evidence-dependent revision. \(\Delta_{\mathrm{revise}}\) must be defined operationally at the chosen site and task; it is **not** a known causal semantic operator. The formula is neither an exact decomposition of the Mamba implementation nor a verbatim archived historical formula. In particular:

- The three terms may be non-identifiable or mathematically/functionally reducible to an existing gated recurrence or reparameterization.
- A learned independent gate does not establish a distinct causal role. Existing Mamba selective carry/write can potentially implement the same function.
- Fixed-size recurrent state is a useful constrained-memory test bed, **not** a guarantee of infinite or lossless memory. Sufficient summary depends on finite precision, task, delay, input distribution, and available external memory.
- One context-time activation update is not automatically persistent parameter learning. Synaptic plasticity and neuromodulated learning rules remain separate, independently falsifiable candidate mechanisms.

## 4. The essential Revise test: dependency-aware functional use

Consider abstract claims \(A\) and \(B\), where \(B\) is a judgment that depends on \(A\). New grounded evidence \(E\) defeats or changes \(A\). Correct updating has two separate endpoints:

\[
\text{Correct State Updating}\not\Rightarrow\text{Correct Functional Use}.
\]

- **State-side question:** Does the model's native state actually change in a way corresponding to replacing the old belief, beyond a linearly decodable signal or an output-layer class bias?
- **Functional-use question:** Does the downstream model use the revised state to reconsider \(B\), while retaining unrelated proposition \(U\)? An output change alone does not prove state-specific mediation.
- **Temporal question:** Does the revision survive delays, intervening text, evidence reordering, and later opportunities to use \(B\)? Does the effect appear at a specified point *before* the endpoint rather than only at a terminal state?
- **Epistemic question:** Did \(E\) actually justify changing \(A\), or should uncertainty and `NOT_ENTITLED` remain? Unsupported contradiction must not force revision.

This causal-dependency diagram is a **task construction hypothesis**, not a claim that a symbolic graph or separate belief module is required.

## 5. Evidence ladder and competing explanations

The shortest credible sequence is: frozen-site **measurement** → held-out temporal **prediction** where appropriate → site- and norm-matched **intervention** → **removal/restoration** for the defined endpoint → independent **functional-use validation** → matched-compute **architectural comparison** only if required.

Predeclare alternatives before introducing new modules:

1. **Output/prior-only explanation:** corrections shift class logits or calibration without meaningful state revision.
2. **Ordinary gated recurrence:** existing selective SSM already implements the purported RFR function, making extra gates redundant.
3. **Distributed representation:** information is maintained or rewritten in multiple changing coordinates; no privileged Retain/Forget/Revise owner exists.
4. **Capacity/compute confound:** improvements arise from more parameters, steps, external memory, or privileged access to evidence.
5. **Readout failure:** state updating may be correct but downstream readout ignores the revised representation.
6. **Spurious short-horizon success:** revisions succeed immediately but fail for delayed derived consequences, held-out relations, or changed evidence order.

Minimum controls should include matched input/evidence, sham vs contradiction, unrelated-fact retention, norm/energy-equated perturbations, class-balanced and pairwise outcomes, delayed and out-of-distribution tests, and frozen tokenizer/model/checkpoint provenance. No post-hoc layer, token, direction, threshold, or favorable subgroup selection may be promoted to a confirmatory discovery.

A weak, null, or negative result should reduce or reject the corresponding *bounded* RFR mechanism claim. It should not trigger unrestricted architecture complexity or metric searches.

## 6. Relationship to existing executed science

The already reported native-state carry/write decomposition, limited PP3/R22 causal effects, seed-dependent correction geometry, and downstream recurrent interference establish useful **motivating observations**. They do **not** demonstrate semantically intelligent Retain, Forget, or Revise operations. In particular:

- The Gen5 Phase2 matched ownership advantage was **not established**.
- G2-P0 shared/full interventions yielded majority-class `NOT_ENTITLED` collapse; average CE gain was not preserved three-class discrimination.
- The exploratory within-pair REFUTE–SUPPORT contrast inversion cannot by itself identify a recurrent-state revision mechanism.
- The G2-H1 layer-22 *output observer* is not a measurement of the raw native recurrent write.

Any future RFR study must retain these negative findings and stage boundaries, not retrospectively reinterpret them as positive proof.

## 7. Long-term interpretation

RFR is one possible bridge from observed recurrent dynamics to the more general research question: **What causal computation lets a bounded-memory intelligence system keep what future judgment needs, suppress what would mislead it, and revise both beliefs and dependent judgments without collateral damage?** Whether that computation requires new architecture, explicit compartments, plastic connections, neuromodulation, extra-state memory, or merely existing recurrent dynamics is open.
