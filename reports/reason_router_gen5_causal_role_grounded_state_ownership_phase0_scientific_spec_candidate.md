## 6. Evidence inheritance contract

Gen5 must inherit positive and negative evidence.

### 6.1 Role over coordinate identity

A role may have different layer-local or scale-local realizations. No fixed 130M coordinate may be declared a universal owner identity.

### 6.2 Depth locality

Failure at one tested depth does not establish global absence. Gen5 must allow layer-local realization and transformation across depth.

### 6.3 Distributed residual mechanism

A dominant component does not exhaust the causal mechanism. Residual causal organization and partner-specific interactions must remain possible.

### 6.4 Objective-conditioned meaning

Keep separate:

1. native causal-role integrity;
2. task-functional readout;
3. behavioral effect.

Success at one level does not imply success at another.

### 6.5 Mechanism is not utility

A valid causal mechanism does not automatically provide useful steering. The failed fixed-mirror steering result remains an inherited boundary.

### 6.6 Native-manifold boundary

Causal efficacy and native-manifold compatibility are distinct. Future Gen5 interventions must not equate a causal effect with native-state plausibility.

### 6.7 Negative evidence remains negative

Gen5 must not silently rescue broad precursor failure, the parked confident-error precursor, non-transported trajectory endpoints, failed historical residual criteria, scale-dependent behavioral bridge, failed fixed-mirror steering, non-universal external transfer, absence of exact geometric invariance, absence of exact additivity, or absence of owner independence.
