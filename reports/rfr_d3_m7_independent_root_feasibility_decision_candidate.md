## 3. Scientific distinction that controls the next phase

1. **Fixed logical task:** All primary examples obey one stipulated rule `B := A AND C` over eight strict Boolean baseline strata. Repeating these strata is necessary for controlled contrasts; core-logic equality is not automatically leakage.
2. **Family ancestry:** A root must be drawn before arm/readout/query/order/paraphrase/alias descendants. Descendants cannot be re-counted as independent families. Unique IDs, SHA256 fingerprints, or source names do not establish new units.
3. **Task-bearing instance diversity:** Distinguish genuine entity/record/attestation configurations from cosmetic renaming and neutral decoration. M6's typed-graph non-isomorphism is a narrow *necessary screen*, not sufficient proof that downstream task behavior or sampling processes meaningfully differ. Attestation-hop count/shape alone may still be a superficial cue.
4. **Independent generation evidence:** Reproducible frozen generator version, sampling frame, root-level substreams/counters, all-attempt ledger including rejected draws, per-component lineage, and independent audit of actual output are required. A metadata declaration containing these names remains `PLAN_ONLY`.
5. **Cross-split claims:** No split currently exists. With only two renderer domains, three mutually disjoint renderer sets for train/dev/test are impossible. A later grouped within-renderer split must be honestly reported with `holdout_contract=NONE`; it cannot establish renderer-family holdout.
6. **Evaluation criteria:** The existing rule-agnostic/C-omission diagnostic can reach high pooled accuracy (M2 reported 3,168/3,456 = 91.67%). A later evaluation must primarily use *paired and conditional* A/B/U readouts by C, arm and time, including `C=F` dependency blocking, not pooled accuracy as a standalone scientific conclusion. These are heuristic diagnostics, not trained-model evidence.
