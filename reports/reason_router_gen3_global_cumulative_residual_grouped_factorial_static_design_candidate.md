## 3. Primary Hypothesis

The next bounded hypothesis is:

GLOBAL_CUMULATIVE_AUTHORIZATION_MODE

This design tests whether attenuation across multiple topology groups is
associated with reproducible authorization-side and final
SUPPORT-versus-NOT_ENTITLED boundary movement in FROZEN51 rows; it does not
presume that such attenuation is necessary or causally sufficient.

This hypothesis is deliberately weaker than claims of:

- one-edge causation
- one-pair causation
- one target-specific blocker mechanism
- polarity-only failure
- native Mamba recurrent-state ownership
- parameter-level gradient orthogonality

## 4. Competing Explanations

The future design must distinguish four possibilities.

MACRO_GROUP_SUFFICIENT:

One topology macro-group alone reproduces cross-seed frozen-51 stable IDs and
the authorization/final-boundary geometry.

CROSS_GROUP_CUMULATIVE_INTERACTION:

A two-macro-group condition creates recurrent frozen-51 breakage beyond the
union of its two constituent macro-group break sets.

GLOBAL_ONLY_CUMULATIVE_THRESHOLD:

No proper macro-group subset reproduces the recurrent frozen-51 residual, while
the existing all-group GLOBAL-HALF D1 condition does.

MIXED_GROUP_AND_GLOBAL_COMPONENT:

Some frozen-51 rows are reproducibly explained by one- or two-group conditions,
while another recurrent subset remains unique to all-group GLOBAL-HALF.

No arbitrary numerical cutoff is introduced for these categories.

## 8. Primary Row-Level Outcome

Aggregate accuracy is not the primary endpoint.

For each seed, define:

A0_BREAK_X = rows correct under matched A0 and wrong under condition X.

FROZEN51 = matched D1 A0-correct breaks absent from every single-edge arm.

The primary outcome for every macro-group condition X is:

A0_BREAK_X intersect FROZEN51.

The exact stable IDs and seed-specific occurrences must be reported.

Cross-seed recurrence must use the same stable ID in at least two frozen
training seeds.

No scientific conclusion may be based only on total break count.

## 11. Falsification Conditions

GLOBAL_CUMULATIVE_AUTHORIZATION_MODE is weakened if a proper macro-group subset
alone reproducibly recovers recurrent FROZEN51 stable IDs under the defined two-of-three-seed criterion without needing
cross-group combination.

It is also weakened if future frozen-51-overlap rows do not reproduce the
authorization/final-boundary movement observed in the historical D1 residual.

MACRO_GROUP_SUFFICIENT is rejected if no U, Q, or D single-group condition
reproduces recurrent frozen-51 rows.

CROSS_GROUP_CUMULATIVE_INTERACTION is rejected for a specific pair of groups if
its frozen-51 overlap is fully accounted for by the union of the two
constituent macro-group conditions.

GLOBAL_ONLY_CUMULATIVE_THRESHOLD is supported only if all proper subsets fail
to reproduce recurrent frozen-51 rows while historical U+Q+D / D1 retains the
recurrent residual.

No category establishes a native Mamba state mechanism.
